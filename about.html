The Beginning

Born to Empower.

Sorielle was founded on a simple yet profound belief: that beauty is not a standard to be met, but a form of self-expression to be celebrated. In a world of filtered perfection, we sought to create a brand that honors the raw, the real, and the radiant.

Our journey started in the vibrant heart of India, inspired by the diverse landscapes and the even more diverse complexions that call this land home.

Inclusion

Celebrating Every Hue.

Indian skin is a masterpiece of depth and richness. From the golden sands of Rajasthan to the deep cocoas of the south, every tone deserves to be seen, celebrated, and complemented.

We meticulously craft our shades to ensure that whether you are fair, olive, deep, or anywhere in between, Sorielle feels like it was made just for you. Because it was.
