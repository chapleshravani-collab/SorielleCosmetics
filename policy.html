Returns & Exchanges

At Sorielle, we strive to ensure you are completely satisfied with your purchase. However, due to the hygiene nature of cosmetic products, we have established the following guidelines:

7-Day Return Window

You can request a return or exchange within **7 days** of receiving your order.

  • Products must be in their original, unopened packaging.
  • The safety seal must be intact.
  • Used or opened products are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected by our quality control team, we will process your refund.

Timeline

Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**.

Shipping Claims

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us at **support@soriellecosmetics.com** within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. We will prioritize your replacement or refund.
